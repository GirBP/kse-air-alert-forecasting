I initially tried to forecast daily alert load, but the archive could not justify complete observation, especially on days without recorded alerts. I changed the target to the duration of registered closed Kyiv events. A regularized model offered only a small, uncertain improvement, so I retained the median of the last thirty completed alerts. The older source ended in September; a separate, verified mirror enabled an October experiment with its own history. The final version is more current, reproducible, and explicit about leakage, missing records, and publication-time uncertainty. Large errors remain visible rather than being removed to improve the results.
